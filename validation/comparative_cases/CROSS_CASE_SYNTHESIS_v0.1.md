# 1. Purpose

This synthesis evaluates what remains after five frozen cases spanning:

$$
Present\ Normal
\rightarrow
Present\ Edge
\rightarrow
Transition
\rightarrow
Agentic/Multi\text{-}Agent
\rightarrow
Future\ Regime\ Proxy
$$

The objective is not to prove IDOS correct.

The objective is to determine:

1. what IDOS adds beyond strong conventional analysis;
2. when IDOS becomes more useful;
3. which IDOS concepts survive across regimes;
4. whether any case-level IV-3 findings can be confirmed;
5. whether MASTER_MAP v1.1 remains structurally viable.

# 3. Cross-Case Pattern Extraction

## 3.1 V001 — Performance

V001 was dominated by conventional productivity and decision-support analysis.

The strongest surviving IDOS distinction was:

$$
\boxed{
Current\ Performance
\neq
Future\ Updateability
}
$$

AI assistance may improve current task performance without establishing that future independent revision capacity is preserved.

---

## 3.2 V002 — Coordination

V002 shifted the problem toward infrastructure-scale coordination.

A key distinction became:

$$
\boxed{
Human\ Presence
\neq
Meaningful\ Oversight
}
$$

and:

$$
Verification
\neq
Influence
\neq
Update
$$

The important issue was no longer only whether humans remained in the loop, but whether they could meaningfully verify, influence, or redesign the system.

---

## 3.3 V003 — Interpretation

V003 introduced machine-side interpretation of human intent.

The central distinction was:

$$
\boxed{
Formal\ Authority
\neq
Effective\ Interpretive\ Authority
}
$$

A human may retain goal-setting authority while the machine increasingly determines how that goal becomes operational meaning.

---

## 3.4 V004 — Distributed Knowing

V004 introduced a distributed multi-agent architecture in which no single actor necessarily contains the full state or trajectory of the system.

The strongest candidate was:

$$
\boxed{
Global\ Output
\neq
Global\ Observer
}
$$

A single system output may exist even when no single agent fully observes the entire process that generated it.

---

## 3.5 V005 — Conditions of Future Change

V005 moved beyond distributed coordination toward multi-level social and institutional dynamics.

The strongest structure was:

$$
\boxed{
Update_t
\rightarrow
Conditions(Update_{t+1})
}
$$

and:

$$
\boxed{
Global\ System_t
\neq
Global\ System_{t+1}
}
$$

The system may update not only its state, but also its future boundary, measurement conditions, and revision possibilities.

# 6. Strongest Cross-Case Patterns

## 6.1 Concepts that activated with regime complexity

The clearest activation pattern was:

$$
N0
\rightarrow
N1
\rightarrow
N2
$$

for:

- Dynamic Boundary;
- Reference Frame;
- Provenance;
- CFV;
- Power.

These dimensions were weak or unnecessary in V001, became helpful in V002, and became structurally necessary by V003.

They then remained N2 through V004 and V005.

This suggests that these concepts become more relevant once:

- role boundaries move;
- interpretation shifts across actors;
- provenance becomes distributed;
- verification becomes relational;
- authority no longer aligns with formal role.

---

## 6.2 Non-monotonic activation

Temporal Compatibility did not increase monotonically:

$$
N0
\rightarrow
N2
\rightarrow
N1
\rightarrow
N2
\rightarrow
N1
$$

This is important.

The five-case pattern does not support:

$$
Regime\ Complexity\uparrow
\Rightarrow
Every\ IDOS\ Concept\uparrow
$$

Different concepts activate under different structural conditions.

This reduces the plausibility that the N2 classifications were mechanically assigned to later cases.

# 8. Why Trajectory Survived

Each case required more than a static-state analysis.

### V001

Current productivity did not reveal long-term skill trajectory.

### V002

Current coordination performance did not reveal dependence or redesign trajectory.

### V003

Current delegation did not reveal the trajectory of interpretive authority.

### V004

Current output did not reveal the distributed process trajectory that generated it.

### V005

Current social state did not reveal institutional and rule trajectory.

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
Static\ State
\rightarrow
Trajectory
}
$$

was consistently necessary.

---

# 9. Why Future Updateability Survived

Future Updateability remained important while its object changed across regimes.

### V001

Human independent revision capacity.

### V002

System redesign capacity.

### V003

Reallocation of interpretive authority.

### V004

Redesign of delegation architecture.

### V005

Modification of future update conditions at institutional or society level.

Thus:

$$
Human
\rightarrow
System
\rightarrow
Authority
\rightarrow
Architecture
\rightarrow
Institution / Society
$$

The recurring question was:

$$
\boxed{
Can\ the\ conditions\ of\ future\ revision\ still\ be\ revised?
}
$$

# 24. Social Implementation Implication

The pilot suggests a practical diagnostic flow.

Begin with:

### Q1

$$
What\ is\ updating?
$$

### Q2

$$
What\ trajectory\ is\ being\ created?
$$

### Q3

$$
Is\ Future\ Updateability\ increasing\ or\ decreasing?
$$

Then activate additional dimensions only if structurally necessary.

Examples:

- moving role or system boundary → Dynamic Boundary;
- machine-side interpretation → Reference Frame / Provenance;
- multi-agent interaction → CFV / MU;
- changing governance or rules → Power / Measurement / 12PDM.

Thus IDOS may be operationalized as:

$$
\boxed{
Adaptive\ Diagnostic\ Architecture
}
$$

rather than a fixed universal checklist.

> IDOSの追加価値は、個別現象を新しく説明することよりも、系の複雑化に応じて「何が更新されるか」「誰が更新できるか」「何を検証できるか」「将来の更新可能性そのものがどう変わるか」を共通構造で追跡できることにある。
