# 2. Stage A — Theory-Neutral Reconstruction

## 2.1 Objective

Stage A reconstructed the historical and empirical case before applying IDOS terminology.

The purpose was to establish:

> What happened, who was involved, what changed, what was measured, what followed, and what remained uncertain.

No IDOS concepts were assigned during this stage.

## 2.2 Historical Setting

Catheter-related bloodstream infections were a recognized safety problem in intensive care units.

Evidence-based preventive practices already existed before the Michigan Keystone project. The central issue was therefore not simply the absence of medical knowledge, but the unreliable implementation of known practices across real clinical environments.

The Michigan Health and Hospital Association Keystone Center for Patient Safety and Quality, Johns Hopkins researchers, and participating hospitals organized a statewide ICU safety initiative.

The project involved more than a checklist. It included:

- evidence-based catheter-insertion and maintenance practices;
- central-line carts and material availability;
- checklist use;
- authority to stop non-compliant procedures in non-emergency situations;
- daily review of catheter necessity;
- infection-rate feedback;
- leadership involvement;
- safety-culture work;
- additional ICU quality-improvement activities;
- statewide collaborative coordination.

## 2.3 Primary Outcome

The original study analyzed 103 ICUs, 1,981 ICU-months, and 375,757 catheter-days.

The reported median catheter-related bloodstream infection rate fell from approximately:

\[
2.7
\]

per 1,000 catheter-days at baseline to:

\[
0
\]

within three months after implementation.

The mean rate fell from approximately:

\[
7.7
\]

at baseline to:

\[
1.4
\]

at 16–18 months.

Later follow-up reported persistence of low infection rates.

## 2.4 Theory-Neutral Reconstruction

A minimally interpretive sequence was:

```text
Known infection-prevention evidence existed
        ↓
Recommended practices were not uniformly embedded in routine ICU work
        ↓
A statewide collaborative was organized
        ↓
Participating ICUs formed local implementation structures
        ↓
Specific evidence-based practices were selected
        ↓
Work processes and material conditions were modified
        ↓
Checklist / supply / stopping / removal / feedback mechanisms were introduced
        ↓
Safety-culture and other ICU interventions occurred concurrently
        ↓
Infection data were collected and fed back
        ↓
Reported infection rates declined substantially
        ↓
Low reported infection rates persisted
```

The arrows above represent temporal and documented relationships, not proof of causal necessity.

## 2.5 Stage A Constraint

The case could not establish the independent causal contribution of:

- checklist use;
- individual clinical practices;
- leadership;
- safety culture;
- infection-rate feedback;
- statewide collaboration;
- secular infection-control trends.

Therefore:

> A complex statewide quality-improvement intervention was followed by a large and sustained reduction in reported catheter-related bloodstream infection rates, but the available evidence does not cleanly decompose the observed change into individual causal mechanisms.

Stage A was frozen on this basis.

# 7. Final Case Assessment

CASE-P003 does not validate IDOS.

It also does not contradict the frozen architecture.

Its main value is methodological.

The case shows that:

1. the minimal update structure can be mapped without substantial theoretical completion;
2. several lower-level IDOS constructs are not required to describe the case;
3. existing implementation and quality-improvement frameworks remain strong competitors;
4. retrospective case fit is not enough to establish explanatory value;
5. future IDOS tests should focus increasingly on incremental measurement and prediction;
6. one especially important future target is whether an intervention changes not only current performance but the system's future **updateability**.

The primary forward-looking question emerging from P003 is therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Can future Updateability be measured independently of current outcome improvement?}
}
\]
