# Intelligence Dynamics Architecture
## Dynamics Model v1.0

**A Provisional Dynamics Model for Residual Accumulation, Holding, Update, and Phase Transition**

---

## 1. Status of This Document

This document describes a provisional dynamics model associated with Intelligence Dynamics Architecture (IDOS).

It should be read together with:

**CANONICAL_v1.0.md**

The distinction is important:

> **Architecture ≠ Dynamics Model ≠ Implementation**

The Canonical defines the conceptual architecture.

This document explores candidate mechanisms through which some of those dynamics may occur.

The reference implementation provides executable representations of selected parts of those mechanisms.

None of the equations in this document are presented as established laws of intelligence or nature.

Unless otherwise stated, the mechanisms described here should be treated as:

> **[P] Provisional**

They are intended to make the architecture explicit enough to be criticized, simulated, operationalized, tested, revised, or rejected.

## 2. From Reality Contact to Dynamic Update

The Canonical describes the following minimal sequence:

**Reality → Observation → Difference → Residual → Holding → Update → Trajectory**

The Dynamics Model focuses primarily on the middle of this sequence:

**Residual  
→ Weighting  
→ Accumulation / Holding  
→ Threshold Dynamics  
→ Update Depth  
→ Trajectory Change**

The central question is:

> **Under what conditions does a Difference remain locally absorbable, and under what conditions does accumulated Residual contribute to deeper transformation?**

IDOS does not assume that every Difference should cause an Update.

Nor does it assume that every Residual should immediately transform a Reference Frame.

A dynamic model therefore requires mechanisms for:

- weighting,
- accumulation,
- persistence,
- Holding,
- thresholding,
- escalation,
- and transition.

---

## 3. Residual

Let a Residual at time \(t\) be represented conceptually as:

\[
R_t
\]

A Residual is not simply the magnitude of an error.

Its dynamic importance may depend on multiple properties.

Two candidate properties are:

- **Precision** \(\pi_t\)
- **Salience** \(S_t\)

Conceptually:

\[
R_t \rightarrow (R_t,\pi_t,S_t)
\]

### Precision

Precision represents the estimated reliability, confidence, or evidential quality associated with the Residual.

A large mismatch generated by unreliable observation may deserve less influence than a smaller but highly reliable contradiction.

### Salience

Salience represents the contextual importance or relevance of the Residual to the current Updating Unit, Reference Frame, objective, boundary, or trajectory.

Precision and Salience are not assumed to be the only relevant variables.

Their operational definitions may differ across systems.

---

## 4. Residual Accumulation

A single Residual does not necessarily justify deep transformation.

Residuals may accumulate over time.

Let:

\[
\rho_t
\]

represent a provisional accumulated Residual state.

A general update form may be written as:

\[
\rho_{t+1}
=
f(\rho_t,R_t,\pi_t,S_t,H_t,\mathcal{T}_{0:t},\ldots)
\]

where:

- \(\rho_t\) = previous accumulated Residual state,
- \(R_t\) = current Residual,
- \(\pi_t\) = Precision,
- \(S_t\) = Salience,
- \(H_t\) = state associated with Holding,
- \(\mathcal{T}_{0:t}\) = relevant Trajectory history.

The function \(f\) is intentionally unspecified.

No universal accumulation function is currently claimed.

Different Updating Units may accumulate, decay, suppress, amplify, reinterpret, or retain Residual differently.

This is an empirical and implementation-level research problem.

---

## 5. Holding

Holding is a Canonical concept.

It refers to:

> **the temporary preservation of unresolved Residual without forcing immediate explanation, optimization, elimination, or closure.**

Holding should not be interpreted as simple inactivity.

A held Residual may remain dynamically available while:

- additional Reality contact occurs,
- alternative explanations emerge,
- other Residuals accumulate,
- competing Reference Frames are explored,
- trajectories interact,
- or a new Possibility Space becomes available.

Conceptually:

\[
Residual
\rightarrow
Holding
\rightarrow
Possible\ Reinterpretation
\]

Holding may therefore affect the future influence of Residual on the dynamics.

However:

> **The relationship between Holding and Residual accumulation has not yet been uniquely operationalized.**

It remains an open research problem whether Holding should be represented as:

- a memory state,
- delayed update,
- uncertainty buffer,
- competing hypothesis set,
- unresolved constraint,
- latent trajectory branch,
- or another mechanism.

The Dynamics Model therefore preserves Holding explicitly without prematurely reducing it to one mathematical implementation.

## 6. Dynamic Threshold

Accumulated Residual may eventually reach conditions under which the currently operative mode of update becomes insufficient.

A minimal candidate representation is:

\[
\rho_t \geq \theta_t
\]

where:

- \(\rho_t\) = accumulated Residual state,
- \(\theta_t\) = dynamic threshold.

The threshold is not assumed to be constant.

Conceptually:

\[
\theta_t
=
g(
ReferenceFrame_t,
Trajectory_t,
Boundary_t,
Context_t,
Resources_t,
History_t,
\ldots
)
\]

This means that the same Residual pattern may produce different responses in different trajectories or contexts.

The threshold model is provisional.

It is intended to express one idea:

> **Deep Update may depend not only on the existence of Residual, but on its history, weighting, persistence, and relation to the current structure.**

---

## 7. Ignition and Phase Transition

When accumulated dynamics can no longer be sufficiently absorbed by the current updating regime, a deeper transition may occur.

This candidate event is referred to as:

> **Ignition**

[P]

Conceptually:

\[
\rho_t \geq \theta_t
\rightarrow
Ignition
\]

Ignition does not necessarily mean catastrophic failure.

It refers to a possible transition from one mode of updating to another.

The deeper interpretation is:

\[
Quantitative\ Accumulation
\rightarrow
Qualitative\ Transition
\]

However, IDOS does not currently claim that all Frame transformations or higher-order Updates are threshold phenomena.

Other mechanisms may exist.

The threshold-and-Ignition model is therefore one candidate dynamics model rather than a universal law.

---

## 8. Update Depth: L1–L5

Not all Updates occur at the same structural depth.

IDOS provisionally distinguishes five levels.

### L1 — Local Absorption

Residual is handled without materially changing the operative model or Reference Frame.

Examples may include:

- noise filtering,
- local correction,
- exception handling,
- temporary adjustment.

Conceptually:

\[
Residual \rightarrow Local\ Absorption
\]

### L2 — Parameter Update

The existing model remains operative, but parameters within it are changed.

\[
Parameters_t
\rightarrow
Parameters_{t+1}
\]

The underlying Reference Frame remains substantially stable.

### L3 — Frame Transformation

The operative Reference Frame itself changes.

\[
F_t
\rightarrow
F_{t+1}
\]

The system does not merely adjust values inside the existing frame.

It changes part of the structure through which Reality is interpreted.

### L4 — Frame Traversal

The Updating Unit moves between distinct available Reference Frames.

\[
F_i
\rightarrow
F_j
\]

This differs from modifying a single frame.

Multiple frames may coexist, compete, or become context-dependent.

### L5 — Possibility-Space Transformation / Generation

The space of frames or possibilities available to the Updating Unit itself changes.

Conceptually:

\[
\mathbb{F}_{existing}
\rightarrow
\mathbb{F}_{expanded}
\]

or, more strongly:

\[
F_{new}\notin\mathbb{F}_{existing}
\]

This is the strongest and most uncertain level.

Whether genuinely new frames or Possibility Spaces can be distinguished from sufficiently complex recombinations of existing structures remains an open question.

Therefore:

> **L5 is a research hypothesis, not an established category of cognition.**

---

## 9. Update Depth Is Not Automatically Hierarchical Value

L1–L5 should not be interpreted as a ranking from inferior to superior intelligence.

A deeper Update is not automatically better.

For many situations:

> **L1 may be the correct response.**

Repeatedly transforming Reference Frames in response to ordinary noise would be unstable.

Conversely, indefinitely absorbing structurally important Residual at L1 or L2 may create frame lock-in.

The relevant question is therefore not:

> **How deep can the system update?**

but:

> **What depth of Update is appropriate to the structure of the Residual and the Reality encountered?**

This creates a regulation problem.

Too little updating may produce rigidity.

Too much updating may produce instability.

## 10. From Update to Trajectory

An Update changes more than the immediate state when it becomes part of history.

Let an intelligence-related state be represented as:

\[
\mathcal{I}_t
\]

A minimal conceptual update can be written as:

\[
\mathcal{I}_{t+1}
=
\mathcal{U}
(
\mathcal{I}_t,
Res_t,
\mathcal{T}_{0:t}
)
\]

where:

- \(\mathcal{U}\) = unspecified update process,
- \(Res_t\) = relevant Residual,
- \(\mathcal{T}_{0:t}\) = prior Trajectory.

Similarly, a Reference Frame may change according to:

\[
\mathcal{F}_{t+1}
=
\mathcal{G}
(
\mathcal{F}_t,
Res_t,
\mathcal{T}_{0:t}
)
\]

These equations are intentionally incomplete.

Their purpose is to make explicit that:

> **Update may be path-dependent.**

The same Residual encountered by two systems with different trajectories may produce different Updates.

---

## 11. Trajectory Representation

A minimal Trajectory representation may be written as:

\[
\mathcal{T}_{0:t}
=
\{
\mathcal{I}_{\tau},
\mathcal{F}_{\tau},
Res_{\tau},
\mathcal{A}_{\tau}
\}_{\tau=0}^{t}
\]

where:

- \(\mathcal{I}_{\tau}\) = state,
- \(\mathcal{F}_{\tau}\) = Reference Frame,
- \(Res_{\tau}\) = Residual,
- \(\mathcal{A}_{\tau}\) = action or intervention.

This is not intended as a complete representation of Trajectory.

Possible future additions include:

- Boundary,
- Updating Unit identity,
- Coupling state,
- Holding state,
- Possibility Space,
- provenance,
- causal continuity,
- environmental response.

Trajectory representation itself remains an open research problem.

---

## 12. Action and Reality Re-contact

Updates do not remain purely internal.

They may generate action.

Conceptually:

\[
\mathcal{A}_t
=
\Pi(
\mathcal{I}_t,
\mathcal{F}_t,
\mathcal{T}_{0:t}
)
\]

Action then changes or probes the relation with Reality.

A minimal environmental representation is:

\[
Reality_{t+1}
\sim
\mathcal{E}
(
Reality_t,
\mathcal{A}_t,
\ldots
)
\]

This creates a recursive loop:

**Reality  
→ Residual  
→ Holding / Accumulation  
→ Update  
→ Action  
→ Reality Re-contact**

New Difference may then emerge.

Therefore, the dynamics are not intended to terminate in a final internal solution.

They return to Reality.

## 13. Trajectory Transition

Canonical v1.0 does not assume that Trajectories remain fixed.

The Dynamics Model therefore needs to accommodate transitions such as:

**Birth  
→ Persistence  
→ Coupling  
→ Branching  
→ Transition  
→ Dormancy  
→ Dissolution  
→ Re-emergence**

This sequence is not universal.

The important point is that Trajectory itself may undergo qualitative transformation.

Three measurement problems follow.

### Phase Signature [O]

What observable pattern indicates the current dynamic regime of a Trajectory?

### Transition Configuration [O]

What configuration of Residual, coupling, history, environment, boundary, or Reference Frame precedes a transition?

### Causal Continuity Pattern [O]

What justifies treating a transformed or re-emergent dynamic process as the continuation of an earlier Trajectory?

These problems are currently open.

---

## 14. Coupling and Higher-Order Updating Units

Multiple Trajectories may interact.

Conceptually:

\[
\{T_1,T_2,\ldots,T_n\}
\rightarrow
Coupling
\]

Some forms of sufficiently persistent or mutually constraining coupling may contribute to the enactment of a higher-order Updating Unit.

\[
Coupling
\rightarrow
Enactment
\rightarrow
Updating\ Unit
\]

However:

> **Coupling alone does not imply Agency.**

Nor does interaction alone imply the emergence of a higher-order Updating Unit.

The conditions under which coupling becomes sufficiently organized to justify higher-order description remain open.

No universal enactment threshold is currently claimed.

---

## 15. Dynamic Boundaries

If Updating Units emerge dynamically, their boundaries may also change.

\[
Boundary_t
\neq
Boundary_{t+1}
\]

Boundary dynamics may affect:

- which Residuals are treated as internal or external,
- which processes belong to the Updating Unit,
- which histories count as part of its Trajectory,
- how responsibility is attributed,
- and what actions become available.

Boundary transformation may therefore interact with Residual accumulation and Update depth.

This relationship is currently under-specified.

It is an important target for future operationalization.

---

## 16. Reference Frames and Possibility Spaces

The Dynamics Model distinguishes among:

**State Update  
→ Parameter Update  
→ Frame Transformation  
→ Frame Traversal  
→ Possibility-Space Transformation / Generation**

The distinction matters because optimization inside a fixed Possibility Space is not equivalent to transforming the space of possibilities itself.

Conceptually:

\[
Trajectory_t
\rightarrow
ReferenceFrame_t
\rightarrow
PossibilitySpace_t
\]

but subsequent dynamics may produce:

\[
PossibilitySpace_t
\rightarrow
New\ Trajectories
\rightarrow
New\ ReferenceFrames
\rightarrow
PossibilitySpace_{t+1}
\]

Therefore, the space in which updating occurs may itself become part of the dynamics.

This is one of the most speculative parts of the current model.

It requires operational criteria before strong empirical claims can be made.

## 17. Measurement

IDOS does not assume that intelligence dynamics can be captured by a single scalar metric.

Candidate measurement modes include:

### Quantitative Measurement

Magnitude, frequency, duration, accumulation, threshold, coupling strength, or other numerical variables.

### Structural Measurement

Changes in relations, topology, organization, boundaries, or Reference Frames.

### Transitional Measurement

Detection of phase changes, regime shifts, branching, dissolution, or re-emergence.

### Trajectory Measurement

Historical and path-dependent patterns across time.

Conceptually:

\[
Measurement
=
Quantitative
+
Structural
+
Transitional
+
Trajectory
\]

This expression is conceptual rather than mathematical.

A major research problem is determining which measurements are useful for which Updating Units and at which scales.

## 18. Minimal Provisional Dynamics

The current dynamics model can be compressed as:

**Reality Contact  
→ Difference  
→ Residual  
→ Precision / Salience  
→ Holding / Accumulation  
→ Dynamic Threshold  
→ Ignition  
→ Update Depth  
→ Trajectory Change  
→ Action  
→ Reality Re-contact**

with possible deeper transformations involving:

**Updating Unit  
↔ Boundary  
↔ Reference Frame  
↔ Possibility Space**

and possible multi-trajectory dynamics involving:

**Trajectory  
↔ Coupling  
↔ Enactment  
↔ Higher-Order Updating Unit**

All of these relationships remain open to revision.

## 19. What This Model Does Not Yet Specify

Dynamics Model v1.0 does not yet provide universal operational definitions for:

- Residual,
- Precision,
- Salience,
- Holding,
- accumulation,
- threshold,
- Ignition,
- Update depth,
- Frame Transformation,
- Frame Traversal,
- Possibility-Space Generation,
- Coupling,
- Enactment,
- Updating Unit identification,
- Boundary transition,
- Trajectory identity,
- Causal Continuity,
- or Phase Signature.

This incompleteness is intentional.

Prematurely assigning arbitrary numerical rules would make the model appear more precise than the current evidence justifies.

The reference implementation may use illustrative mechanisms.

Such mechanisms should be clearly marked as provisional examples rather than universal dynamics.

---

## 20. Falsifiability and Model Reduction

The Dynamics Model should be revised or reduced if empirical work shows that:

- trajectory history adds no useful information beyond static state,
- Holding provides no distinguishable explanatory role,
- deeper Update levels cannot be operationally distinguished,
- Frame Generation cannot be distinguished from recombination,
- Possibility-Space Generation cannot be meaningfully identified,
- threshold dynamics provide no useful explanatory value,
- or existing models explain the relevant phenomena without additional IDOS constructs.

Therefore:

> **Failure is informative.**

And:

> **The model must be allowed to shrink.**

The purpose of formalization is not to protect the architecture.

It is to expose it to criticism.

---

## 21. Relationship to the Reference Implementation

The accompanying reference code is intended to make selected concepts:

- inspectable,
- executable,
- modifiable,
- and criticizable.

It is not intended to prove that intelligence has been reduced to code.

The implementation should distinguish clearly among:

- Canonical definitions,
- provisional mechanisms,
- illustrative parameter choices,
- and unresolved interfaces.

In particular, arbitrary rules such as:

`if coupling > 0.8: agency = True`

should not be interpreted as IDOS theory unless independently justified.

Open criteria should remain open in the implementation.

---

## 22. Closing

The purpose of the Dynamics Model is not to provide a final equation of intelligence.

It is to make one family of candidate dynamics explicit.

At its center is a simple possibility:

> **Intelligence may depend not only on whether a system can reduce error, but on how it preserves, accumulates, interprets, and transforms unresolved Difference across time.**

That process may alter:

- state,
- parameters,
- Reference Frames,
- boundaries,
- Updating Units,
- Trajectories,
- and Possibility Spaces.

None are assumed permanently fixed.

Yet the dynamics remain constrained by repeated contact with Reality.

> **Nothing is permanently fixed.  
> Everything remains answerable to Reality.**
