## Central proposition

Before an agency, meaning, boundary, or reference frame is established, interactions may give rise to differentiations, patterns, boundaries, reference frames, agency, and representation. Emergence is contingent: differences and later stages may fail to arise.

## Upper diagram: possible formation sequence (0–7)

| Label | Diagram term | Preserved distinction |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Interaction Field | A field of interaction not yet segmented into difference, subject or meaning. |
| 1 | Fluctuation / Asymmetry | Perturbation, instability, unevenness; fluctuation is not sufficient to guarantee difference. |
| 2 | Difference Emergence | A possibility of differentiation and transient stabilization, not automatic representation. |
| 3 | Pattern Stabilization | Local patterns, recurrence, synchronization or repetition can arise. |
| 4 | Boundary Genesis & Self-Maintenance | Boundary emergence and self-maintaining operations; autopoiesis is one possible mode. |
| 5 | Reference Frame Formation | Emergence of a way of observing and distinguishing a world. |
| 6 | Agency Emergence | Possibility of organized acting/goal-directed agency, embodied or environmental. |
| 7 | Representation / Symbol | Articulation into concepts, language, theories or models. |

**Non-equivalences:** difference emergence is not the same as naming; boundary formation does not by itself establish sovereign agency; representation does not exhaust the prior domain.

## Middle diagram: structural coupling and operational closure

The environment / others perturb a system through **structural coupling**. Perturbation does **not** directly dictate the system's internal state. Internal dynamics unfold within an operationally closed, self-producing organization (an autopoietic unit as a **possible mode**, not a universal definition of intelligence). The system acts upon its environment, the environment changes, and further perturbations may follow. The relationships are recursive, not one-way causal instructions from the environment.

## Lower diagram: pre-representational residual and articulation

The original figure distinguishes a *pre-representational residual* and three diagnostic layers:

- **R1 — Representational Residual:** a difference that existing descriptions or knowledge cannot adequately explain.
- **R2 — Reference-Frame Residual:** a difference that existing reference-frame structure cannot adequately capture.
- **R3 — Boundary Residual:** something that calls into question the delimitation of the subject/system and its environment.

The figure places **Holding**, **Pattern Stabilization**, and an **Articulation Gate** between pre-representational dynamics and possible articulate outcomes. Holding includes temporal allowance for tracking and exploration rather than immediately forcing a category. The articulation gate is conditioned by factors represented in the figure (including F, H, B, E, T and R); they are not asserted as universal numerical thresholds.

Four different outcomes remain distinct:

1. **Captured:** articulable and captured as representationally available difference.
2. **Uncaptured / Persistent:** not yet articulated, but some trace or interaction continues.
3. **Suppressed / Latent:** a potentially articulable trace remains latent or suppressed; absence from current representation does not prove absence.
4. **Irrecoverable (from the current system):** unavailable to the present system's means of recovery. **This is not equivalent to absolute metaphysical irreversibility.**

The two-axis representation **Articulability × Trace Persistence** must remain distinct: whether something can be articulated and whether a trace survives are different questions. Reactivation and re-articulation remain possible pathways where applicable.

## Pre-representational characteristics

The diagram names: non-local, pre-linguistic, non-linear, embodied/environmental, and pre-semantic aspects. These characterize the proposal; they are not claims that such processes have already been objectively measured in all domains.

## Overall architecture in the source

The figure situates pre-representational dynamics as a *Layer 0* relative to reference frame formation, observation/reality contact, and update/history/evolution, with recursive feedback and possible emergence of a newly articulated world. It does not assert that any particular image stage must occur or that autopoiesis is a universal definition of intelligence.

## Interpretive constraints

- Do not replace the pre-representational field with an already observed `Difference` or `Residual` object.
- Do not equate `Uncaptured`, `Suppressed`, and `Irrecoverable`.
- Do not equate system operational closure with isolation from the environment.
- Do not convert contingent appearance into a deterministic pipeline.
- Do not merge this figure silently into Discovery Architecture v1.0 or executable IDOS Reference modules.

# ver.56 — Open Questions (Preserved, Not Resolved)

**Status:** questions arising from the original diagram and its preserved Figure 033 audit entry. Items identified as *new research questions* are explicitly separated below. These are not validated claims.

## Questions already preserved in the original audit

1. **Observability before representation:** Under what conditions could a pre-representational phenomenon be observed without presupposing the distinctions whose emergence is under investigation?
2. **Uncaptured versus suppressed:** How can persistent but uncaptured traces be distinguished from suppressed/latent traces without making the current observer's absence of evidence a definitive classification?
3. **Irrecoverability:** What would distinguish absolute irreversibility from inability to reconstruct a trace **from the current system**?
4. **Subthreshold differences:** How should possible differences that never stabilize or cross articulation thresholds be represented, if at all?
5. **Emergence without inevitability:** How do we describe fluctuation and coupling that do **not** yield stable difference, a boundary, a frame or agency?
6. **Comparison with nearby originals:** Cross-check ver.55 and ver.73 before asserting theoretical equivalence; neither should be silently subsumed into ver.56.

## Further questions prompted by the original (not claims made by the figure)

- How can an investigator intervene on coupling conditions without presuming knowledge of the difference they hope will become articulable?
- How can changes in the world and in the observing apparatus be experimentally distinguished when both are revisable?
- What evidence would falsify a claim that an intervention brought forth a new kind of distinction rather than merely detecting a previously available one?

## Evidence discipline

A later observer may identify a trace retrospectively; that does not prove the trace was an already articulated difference at its earlier time. `Captured`, `Uncaptured / Persistent`, `Suppressed / Latent`, and `Irrecoverable (from current system)` should not be used as automatic classifier outputs without domain-specific warrant.
