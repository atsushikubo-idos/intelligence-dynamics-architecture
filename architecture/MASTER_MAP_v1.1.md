# 0. Purpose

This document records the frozen `MASTER_MAP_v1.1` architecture adopted after completion of:

- Future-Regime CASE-F001 through CASE-F010;
- cross-case synthesis;
- construct-level reduction;
- architecture-level rival comparison;
- Red Team reduction;
- reverse-application testing against CASE-F001 through CASE-F010.

The purpose is not to expand IDOS.

The purpose is to identify the smallest architecture that preserves the explanatory and integrative value that repeatedly survived the Future-Regime validation sequence.

The revision is therefore guided by:

$$
\boxed{
Add\ Less
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
Delete\ What\ Is\ Not\ Necessary
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
Preserve\ What\ Transfers
}
$$

`MASTER_MAP_v1.0` remains the prior frozen historical baseline, while this document becomes the current frozen baseline.

Formally:

$$
\boxed{
MASTER\_MAP\ v1.1
=
Frozen\ Research\ Baseline
}
$$

# 1. Ultimate Research Question

The central IDOS question remains:

> **Can heterogeneous intelligences remain different, remain connected without erasing their differences, and transform those differences into continuous updates of the larger intelligence system?**

The Future-Regime validation adds a second-order question:

> **Can the system remain capable of changing the way it updates itself?**

Therefore the revised research center becomes:

$$
\boxed{
How\ do\ heterogeneous\
intelligence\ systems\
change,
become\ observable,
and\ remain\ capable\
of\ changing\
how\ they\ change?
}
$$

# 3. Revision Rationale

## 3.1 Why Revision Is Needed

CASE-F001 through CASE-F010 repeatedly showed that several constructs remained useful but were not necessary as Core primitives.

Most notably:

- Residual remained useful but weak as a necessary Core construct;
- Holding remained domain-relevant but weak as a universal Core construct;
- the full 12PDM repeatedly mapped onto cases but was not required for explanation;
- Continuity increasingly appeared derivable from Trajectory, Provenance, and causal lineage;
- Re-measurability appeared derivable from Measurement, Provenance, and historical accessibility;
- System Update appeared assessable from Updating Unit, Boundary, and Trajectory.

At the same time, the following repeatedly survived across materially different future regimes:

- Updating Unit;
- Dynamic Boundary;
- Trajectory;
- Reference Frame;
- Measurement;
- Provenance;
- Future Updateability.

Relationally, the following also repeatedly survived:

- CFV — Cross-Frame Verifiability;
- MU — Mutual Updateability.

This produces pressure toward a smaller architecture.

---

## 3.2 Strongest Cross-Case Result

The strongest repeated result across the Future-Regime sequence is:

$$
\boxed{
Current\ Success
\neq
Future\ Updateability
}
$$

The form of Current Success differed across cases:

- current AI performance;
- executive decision performance;
- regulatory compliance;
- international coordination;
- disaster-response success;
- safe high-speed operation;
- recursive service performance;
- enterprise performance;
- audit verification success;
- scientific success.

Yet Future Updateability remained analytically distinct.

Therefore Future Updateability is retained as a major architecture candidate.

---

## 3.3 Revision Direction

The revision moves IDOS from a process-centered reading toward an updateability-centered architecture.

Formally:

$$
\boxed{
Process\text{-}Centered
\rightarrow
Updateability\text{-}Centered
}
$$

and:

$$
\boxed{
12PDM\ as\ Core
\rightarrow
12PDM\ as\ Observation/Description\ Protocol
}
$$

The revision also distinguishes:

$$
\boxed{
Dynamics
}
$$

from:

$$
\boxed{
Observability
}
$$

from:

$$
\boxed{
Second\text{-}Order\ Updateability
}
$$

# 4. Proposed v1.1 Architecture

The proposed architecture consists of three primary layers, one relational layer, and cross-cutting constraints.

---

# 4.1 Layer 1 — Dynamics

The Dynamics Layer asks:

> **What is changing, within what boundary, and along what trajectory?**

It contains:

$$
\boxed{
Updating\ Unit
+
Dynamic\ Boundary
+
Trajectory
}
$$

The Dynamics Layer also retains **Difference** as a transition driver rather than as a separate Core layer or entity.

$$
\boxed{
Difference
=
Transition\ Driver
}
$$

A provisional representation is:

$$
D_t
=
Difference(
U_t,
Environment_t,
OtherUnits_t,
Expectation_t
)
$$

IDOS therefore distinguishes:

$$
\boxed{
Change
\neq
Update
}
$$

A provisional definition of Update is:

$$
\boxed{
Update
=
Difference\text{-}responsive\
state/structure\ transition
}
$$

That is, Update occurs when a relevant Difference contributes to a change in state, structure, relation, or update rule.

A provisional first-order update form is:

$$
S_{t+1}
=
G_t(S_t,D_t)
$$

Difference is therefore retained without restoring Residual as a universal Core primitive.

---

## 4.1.1 Updating Unit

$$
\boxed{
U_t
}
$$

The Updating Unit is the unit whose change is being tracked.

Possible Updating Units include:

- Human;
- AI system;
- Human–AI relation;
- organization;
- temporary coalition;
- distributed service;
- institutional regime;
- scientific research system.

The Updating Unit may change:

$$
\boxed{
U_t
\neq
U_{t+1}
}
$$

The Future-Regime cases repeatedly showed:

$$
\boxed{
Updating\ Unit
\neq
Boundary
}
$$

Therefore Updating Unit remains independent.

---

## 4.1.2 Dynamic Boundary

$$
\boxed{
B_t
}
$$

Boundary specifies what is included in the system under analysis.

The same Updating Unit may be evaluated under different boundaries.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
System\ Update
is\ Boundary\text{-}dependent
}
$$

and:

$$
\boxed{
B_t
\neq
B_{t+1}
}
$$

must be allowed.

---

## 4.1.3 Trajectory

$$
\boxed{
T_{0:t}
}
$$

IDOS retains the proposition:

$$
\boxed{
Intelligence
is\ not\ adequately\
represented\ as\ a\ point
}
$$

Trajectory captures change through time rather than only current state.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
Current\ State
\neq
Trajectory
}
$$

and:

$$
\boxed{
Current\ Success
\neq
Healthy\ Trajectory
}
$$

---

# 4.2 Layer 2 — Observability and Reconstruction

The Observability Layer asks:

> **From what frame is change observed, how is it measured, and how can its history be reconstructed?**

It contains:

$$
\boxed{
Reference\ Frame
+
Measurement
+
Provenance
}
$$

---

## 4.2.1 Reference Frame

For observer or intelligence \(i\):

$$
\boxed{
F_i
}
$$

A provisional observation remains expressible as:

$$
Y_i
=
M_i(R;F_i,B_i,\ldots)
$$

Different observers may generate different observations from the same Reality.

$$
F_i
\neq
F_j
$$

may imply:

$$
D_i
\neq
D_j
$$

Reference Frame is not treated as a standalone process node.

It is a cross-cutting epistemic condition on:

- observation;
- measurement;
- interpretation;
- verification.

---

## 4.2.2 Measurement

$$
\boxed{
M_t
}
$$

Measurement is dynamic.

The measurement system itself may change:

$$
\boxed{
M_t
\neq
M_{t+1}
}
$$

Measurement stability does not imply measurement adequacy.

$$
\boxed{
Measurement\ Stability
\neq
Measurement\ Adequacy
}
$$

---

## 4.2.3 Provenance

$$
\boxed{
P_{0:t}
}
$$

Provenance tracks where a state, decision, result, or trajectory came from.

It may include:

- data lineage;
- model versions;
- Human decisions;
- AI outputs;
- tool calls;
- measurement criteria;
- boundary definitions;
- causal lineage;
- transformation history.

Trajectory and Provenance remain distinct.

$$
\boxed{
Trajectory
\neq
Provenance
}
$$

Trajectory answers:

> How did the state change?

Provenance answers:

> Through what origin and causal path did the state arise?

---

# 4.3 Layer 3 — Second-Order Updateability

The third layer asks:

> **Can the system change the way it changes?**

Its central construct is:

$$
\boxed{
Future\ Updateability
}
$$

---

## 4.3.1 Future Updateability

The strongest provisional definition is:

$$
\boxed{
Future\ Updateability
=
Capacity\ to\ revise\
the\ conditions\
of\ future\ revision
}
$$

This includes the capacity to revise:

- Updating Units;
- boundaries;
- measurement systems;
- verification systems;
- Human/AI role allocation;
- governance structures;
- dependency structures;
- system architecture;
- update rules themselves.

If ordinary first-order update is represented as:

$$
S_{t+1}
=
G_t(S_t,D_t)
$$

then Future Updateability asks whether the update operator itself can be revised:

$$
\boxed{
G_t
\rightarrow
G_{t+1}
}
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
First\text{-}Order\ Update
=
Difference\text{-}responsive\ state/structure\ change
}
$$

while:

$$
\boxed{
Second\text{-}Order\ Updateability
=
Capacity\ to\ revise\ the\ update\ operator
}
$$

Therefore Future Updateability may be interpreted as:

$$
\boxed{
Second\text{-}Order\ Updateability
}
$$

and:

$$
\boxed{
Adaptation
\neq
Future\ Updateability
}
$$

\boxed{

\boxed{

$$
\boxed{

Therefore CFV is treated as a relational epistemic capacity.

---

## 5.2 Mutual Updateability — MU

$$
\boxed{
MU_{ij}
}
$$

MU asks:

> Can interaction between units produce meaningful revision in both directions?

The Future-Regime cases repeatedly showed:

$$
\boxed{
Interaction
\neq
Mutual\ Updateability
}
$$

and:

$$
\boxed{
Individual\ Updateability
\neq
Relational\ Updateability
}
$$

It is possible that:

$$
FU_i>0
$$

and:

$$
FU_j>0
$$

while:

$$
MU_{ij}=0
$$

Therefore MU remains a distinct relational capacity.

# 6. Cross-Cutting Conditions and External Grounding

The final Red Team distinguishes between cross-cutting conditions and external grounding.

Cross-cutting conditions modify the effective operation of the architecture:

$$
\boxed{
Power
+
Temporal\ Compatibility
}
$$

Reality Contact is not treated as the same type of condition. It is retained separately as external grounding.

---

## 6.1 Power

Power affects:

- who can observe whom;
- who can deny access;
- who can modify measurement;
- who can change governance;
- who can terminate or constrain another unit;
- whose evidence can produce operational change.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
Epistemic\ Authority
\neq
Operational\ Power
}
$$

Power is treated as a cross-cutting modifier.

---

## 6.2 Temporal Compatibility

Meaningful updateability may depend on relevant timing.

For example:

$$
UpdateSpeed_{AI}
\gg
UpdateSpeed_H
$$

may make nominal Human oversight operationally ineffective.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
Connected
\neq
Temporally\ Interoperable
}
$$

Temporal Compatibility applies across:

- observation;
- verification;
- MU;
- power;
- trajectory.

---

## 6.3 External Grounding — Reality Contact

Agreement is not sufficient.

$$
\boxed{
Consensus
\neq
Reality\ Contact
}
$$

Human–AI systems, multi-AI systems, organizations, and institutions may converge internally while drifting from external Reality.

Reality Contact is therefore retained as **External Grounding** rather than as a cross-cutting modifier. It constrains internally coherent but potentially reality-detached trajectories.
