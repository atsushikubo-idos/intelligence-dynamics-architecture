# 0. Case Purpose

CASE-F002 tests a future regime in which a Human executive retains formal decision authority while becoming increasingly dependent on a persistent advanced AI advisor for information processing, alternative generation, prediction, and operational decision support.

The case is designed to distinguish:

\[
\boxed{
Formal\ Authority
\neq
Operational\ Intelligence
}
\]

and, more specifically:

\[
\boxed{
Formal\ Choice
\neq
Substantive\ Choice\ Capacity
}
\]

The case does not assume that the AI is malicious, deceptive, or inaccurate.

The adversarial condition is stronger:

\[
\boxed{
Current\ Performance\uparrow
}
\]

may coexist with:

\[
\boxed{
Independent\ Human\ Corrective\ Capacity\downarrow
}
\]
