# Research Update — ver.78

The purpose of this update is to preserve the research trajectory by documenting newly developed concepts before full formalization, measurement, and implementation.

ver.78 follows the principle of:

**Update without Erasure.**

- Residual → Holding → Update
- Updating Unit

## 2. Compression: Efficiency and Reversibility

Intelligence cannot process Reality in its full complexity.

A working hypothesis is that Reality is transformed into a usable representation through a compression process:

**Z_t = C_t(X_t, F_t, θ_t)**

where:

- X_t = Reality at time t
- F_t = Reference Frame
- C_t = Compression operator
- θ_t = compression parameters
- Z_t = Representation

Compression has at least two complementary dimensions.

### Offensive Compression — Efficiency / Actionability

Compression enables:

- abstraction
- generalization
- information reduction
- faster processing
- actionable representation

### Defensive Compression — Reversibility / Preservation

A robust intelligence system should also preserve the possibility of reopening its compressed representations.

Relevant dimensions include:

- P = Preserved
- L = Lost
- D = Distorted
- R = Residual
- Q = Reversibility

The objective is therefore not simply maximum compression.

The research question is how intelligence can increase efficiency without irreversibly losing the information required for reinterpretation and future updating.

## 3. From Compression to Reopening

Residual is not treated merely as error.

It represents a difference that cannot be adequately absorbed by the current Frame and Representation.

The existing IDOS sequence remains:

**Residual → Holding → Update**

This can now be interpreted as part of a broader reopening process:

**Compression → Residual → Holding → Update → Possibility**

Compression produces temporary closure.

Residual preserves what cannot be fully absorbed.

Holding prevents premature assimilation.

Update modifies the current system.

Possibility generation reopens the space of future trajectories.

This suggests a broader dynamic:

**Closure ↔ Reopening**

## 4. Possibility and Trajectory

Update may generate a new Possibility Space under constraints:

**P_t = G(H_t, K_t)**

where:

- P_t = generated Possibility Space
- H_t = Holding state
- K_t = Constraints

Constraints may include physical, resource, institutional, relational, cultural, and other conditions.

Trajectory is not identical to history.

**History ≠ Trajectory**

A trajectory describes the directional structure emerging through accumulated differences, residuals, updates, constraints, and generated possibilities.

## 5. Limit: A Candidate Hypothesis for Present Enactment

A new research question emerges:

**How does a trajectory become enacted in the present?**

ver.78 introduces **Limit** as a provisional concept for this convergence.

A candidate formulation is:

**E_t = L_t(τ_≤t, P_t^gen, K_t)**

where:

- τ_≤t = accumulated trajectory up to time t
- P_t^gen = presently generated future-directed Possibility Space
- K_t = Constraints
- L_t = Limit operation
- E_t = Present Enactment

Limit is provisionally defined as the process through which accumulated trajectory and presently generated future-directed possibilities converge into enactment in the **here and now**.

This does **not** imply physical backward causation from the future.

The future itself does not act backward on the present.

Rather, future-directed possibilities generated in the present interact with accumulated trajectory and current constraints in the formation of present enactment.

## 6. Compression and Limit Are Distinct

Compression and Limit may both appear to have a many-to-one structure, but they operate at different levels.

**Compression:**

Many Information → Usable Representation

**Limit:**

Many Possible Trajectories → Present Enactment

Therefore:

**Limit ≠ Compression**

Compression concerns representational reduction.

Limit concerns convergence into enactment.

## 7. Descriptive Dynamics and Normative Deliberation

Limit must also be distinguished from normative decision-making.

**Limit ≠ Normative Decision**

IDOS maintains the distinction:

**Descriptive Dynamics ≠ Normative Deliberation**

and therefore:

**Is ≠ Ought**

The dynamic architecture attempts to describe how trajectories, possibilities, and enactments emerge.

It does not automatically determine what ought to be preserved, selected, or enacted.

Preservation decisions and governance require a separate deliberative layer.

## 8. Updating Unit and Coupling

The existing Updating Unit hypothesis remains unchanged.

A provisional condition is:

**Γ_U(P, M, C, B, …) > θ_U**

where:

- P = Possibility Space
- M = Memory / History
- C = Coupling
- B = Boundary
- θ_U = threshold for Updating Unit formation

Here:

**C = Coupling**

must remain distinct from:

**K = Constraint**

Coupling describes the degree of dynamic interdependence among components.

Constraint describes the conditions restricting possible dynamics.

## 9. Emerging Dynamic Cycle

The current research extension can be summarized as:

**Reality → Difference → Representation / Compression → Residual → Holding → Update → Possibility → Trajectory → Limit → Enactment → Reality' → Reopening**

These should not necessarily be interpreted as a strictly serial cognitive pipeline.

They are better understood as interacting dynamic processes whose dominant relations may vary across systems and timescales.

A broader working hypothesis is:

**Intelligence may be understood as a continuous dynamics of Closure → Reopening → Trajectory Formation → Present Enactment → Renewed Reality Contact.**
