# Scope and Limitations — IDOS v0.49 English Research Preview

## Demonstrated

- Five heterogeneous **synthetic** updating units with distinct reference axes and transition rules.
- Deterministic consent-aware observation, proposal, decision, contact, boundary change, and synthetic recontact events.
- Time-indexed relation and state visualization, residual statements, provenance, and exported audits.
- Comparison of two explicitly chosen feedback assumptions on identical events.
- Illustrative question/purpose/criterion annotations and a manual Path Closure review list.
- Forty-two scoped software assertions passing in one headless Chromium run.

## Not demonstrated

- A universal dynamic law, new theory empirically confirmed, or added predictive advantage over prior methods.
- Real-world AGI/ASI interaction, validated human or organizational modeling, or independent Reality contact.
- Learning actual goals, interpreting mental states, or predicting future goals or probabilities.
- Measured Future Updateability, automatic Path Closure determination, or canonical DV0.
- Complete conformance with frozen CDP v1.0 or draft CDP v2.0.
- Security, privacy/access controls, performance hardening, or production deployment readiness.
- Complete integration of historical Japanese v0.1–v0.49 experimentation panels.

## Differences from earlier Japanese prototype

This is a **focused English release interface**, not a literal full translation of the earlier 467 KB Japanese HTML. The v0.37/v0.38 transition mechanisms were retained and their human-facing text translated into English. Other historical experiment panels and the richer v0.44 residual-adjudication interface were **not** carried into this English release. Its 42 bounded checks are a newly defined suite; they are **not identical** to the older Japanese v0.49 suite.

## Research integrity

The radar's six display axes do not correspond one-to-one with the six canonical IDOS characteristics. The illustrative purpose annotations do not change the simulator's decisions. Retained residual phrases are not automatically resolved. The future remains unknown; no numerical forecast is calibrated. A simulation that displays changing references has not thereby validated a model of real intelligence.

## Research next steps

Consolidate the legacy kernels and modules; implement a provenance-rich residual adjudication ledger; test against independent observations, baseline models and out-of-sample cases; verify schema conformity; develop access controls before connecting live systems.
